## How games are charged

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D8 | Single frame: fixed price per table (Tables 1, 2, 3, 6 = Rs 250; Tables 4, 5, 7 = Rs 200). When a frame ends, the app asks who lost. The loser pays. The winner is recorded under the player. |
| D9 | Double frame (2 vs 2) on any snooker table: double price, and the two losers pay 50/50. Three players also count as a double; the worker picks the losing side and can adjust the split. |
| D10 | Every frame, including a rematch, is a new frame at full price. The same players playing again and again are shown as one session, e.g. “Frame 4 · Ali 3 – 1 Bilal”. |
| D11 | Century: started under one player’s name, and he pays all. Any number of players, no score kept. The rate per minute shows when starting (default Rs 15 from the price card) and the worker can change it. Whole minutes only: 42 min 35 sec = 42 min. No minimum. |
| D12 | Rooms: under one player’s name. Default Rs 1,500 an hour, the worker can change it when starting (Q8). Minimum 1 hour; after that, by the minute (hourly price ÷ 60 × minutes), rounded to the nearest rupee (Q9). |
| D13 | Pool, foosball, carrom, ludo, PlayStation: charged per game, loser pays. PlayStation is not hourly. Prices set by the owners; until then these games are shown but cannot be started (Q3). |
| D14 | Pause and resume on every game, for power cuts and emergencies. |
| D15 | A frame can be discarded (nobody pays). A fight: marked “Unpaid – dispute” and the owners decide later in Activity. |
| D16 | Game started by mistake: undo within 2 minutes. After that, cancelling needs a reason and owners see every cancellation. |
| D17 | Change game type within 5 minutes of starting. After 5 minutes the worker either ends and charges the current game, or discards it, then starts the new one. |
| D18 | Replace a player in a running frame, and move a running game to another table. |
| D19 | Damage charge (cue, ball, cloth) can be added to a player’s bill with a note. |

## Players and money

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D20 | Every player has an account with name and phone number. Quick add: type a new name when starting a game, the account is made at once, and the phone number is asked for to complete it. |
| D21 | Player search always shows name and phone together, so players with the same name are easy to tell apart. |
| D22 | Each player’s account works like a bank statement: games, canteen, damage charges, payments, advance and credit, with a running balance. Wins, losses and games played are recorded too. |
| D23 | Checkout: old balance + new charges. Pay in full, pay part, or put on credit. One bill can be split across payment methods. |
| D24 | Payment methods: Cash, Bank transfer, Easypaisa, JazzCash; owners can add more accounts. Reports show totals for each method. No transaction IDs. |
| D25 | Credit: any player; the worker decides at checkout. No credit limit, no warnings, no blocking. Credit older than 15 days is shown as old. |
| D26 | Paying more than the bill: the extra becomes advance balance. Players can also pay in advance. |
| D27 | Anyone not checked out when the sheet closes: the bill moves to credit and is marked “Left without checkout”. |
| D28 | Owners enter old credit from the register as opening balances. |
| D29 | No receipts, no printing, no WhatsApp. Every bill stays in the player’s account; owners can open it and download it as PDF any time. |
| D30 | Owners can edit any bill or sheet entry to fix a problem. Every edit is recorded: who, when, old value, new value and reason. |

## Added 12 Oct 2026 (owners’ privacy and workers)

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D49 | **Private owner address.** The owner portal opens only at a secret address that only Aqil, Adil and the developer know (example `stockers.pk/owner-k7m2q9`). The counter screen has no owner sign-in, no owner button and no link; the counter app’s code does not contain the address. Any other address shows a plain “Page not found”. Owners can change the address in Settings (all owner devices are signed out). |
| D50 | **Two workers with their own PINs:** Shabir and Zain. Owners can add a worker, change a PIN or turn a worker’s sign-in off. |
| D51 | **Sign-in times.** Owners set a daily time for each worker (example: Shabir 2:00 AM–10:00 AM, Zain 2:00 PM–11:00 PM). Only the time of day is compared with the real clock, never the date. A time can cross midnight. Outside his time the counter shows “Not your time to sign in” with his time. The server clock decides. Ten minutes before the end the counter warns him; at the end he is signed out and games keep running. |
| D52 | **Every action has a name.** Starting, ending, moving, discarding, discounts, checkouts, canteen sales, damage charges, drawer expenses, closing a sheet, edits and setting changes are saved with who did it (Shabir, Zain, Aqil or Adil) and shown in Activity, sheet history, statements and expenses. |
| D53 | **Drawer expense on the counter.** A worker can record cash taken from the drawer (food, petrol, tea and milk, cleaning, repairs, other) with an amount and a note. It comes off the cash the drawer should have at close. Owners see it in Expenses and Activity with his name and can change or remove it. |

## Removed on purpose (do not build)

- Table bookings, waiting list, deposits
- Printed receipts and printers
- WhatsApp anything
- Credit limits, credit warnings, blocking players
- Players viewing their own statement, QR codes
- One player paying for friends in one checkout
- A head worker (separate worker logins and sign-in times were **added** on 12 Oct)
- Century scores, tournaments, loyalty, TV leaderboard, automatic lights, player screen
- Prices by time of day or for members
